Who we are

A team with port craft serving port authorities and terminals worldwide

DOMINUS is a consulting firm specialized in port system development worldwide. We were founded to address a specific gap: the need for independent technical and institutional advisory, with real port craft and a bias for implementation, both for public port authorities and private terminals.

Our team was built inside the sector: port authority management, terminal operations, regulation and infrastructure planning. That background lets us understand problems from the inside, speak our clients' language and produce solutions that can actually be put in motion — not just polished diagnoses.

Values

The principles that guide our work

Six commitments we make to every client and every project.

Technical independence

Our only product is independent professional judgment. We hold no crossed interests with operators or providers in the sector.

Commitment to the public sphere

When we work with port authorities, we understand we're contributing to the country's port public policy.

Technical rigor

Every recommendation is backed by data, documented methodology and regional benchmarking.

Confidentiality

We handle sensitive information from institutions and operators. Our teams operate under strict confidentiality protocols.

Implementation focus

We don't deliver decorative reports. Every project reaches an action plan and, when the client asks, all the way to execution.

Long-term relationships

We build lasting relationships with our clients, beyond any single project.
